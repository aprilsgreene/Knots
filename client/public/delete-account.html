Delete your Knots account

You can permanently delete your Knots account at any time. This removes your sign-in and everything you have saved.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Knots and sign in.
  2. Tap Settings at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read the message, then tap Delete my account to confirm.
Open Settings

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in (your email address and password).
  • Every relationship, note, check-in, rating, and tag you created.
  • Your app settings and preferences.

Deletion happens right away and cannot be undone. We do not keep a copy.

Cannot sign in

Email support@kinlight.app from the address you signed up with and write "Delete my account" in the subject line. We will confirm and delete your account and data within 30 days.
